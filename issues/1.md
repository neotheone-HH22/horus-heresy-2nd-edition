### Title
Missing definition for Master of Armour Warlord Trait in Armoured Spearhead (Kratos, Sicaran, Predator)

### Description
**Describe the issue**  
The Armoured Spearhead Rite of War in Panoptica allows a single Sicaran, Predator, or Kratos to be taken as an HQ with the Master of Armour Warlord Trait and must be the army's Warlord. However, the detailed entry for the "Master of Armour" Warlord Trait is missing from the documentation. The Kratos must have a defined Warlord Trait entry to clarify its rules, bonuses, and any unique effects.  

**Suggested fix**  
- Add a proper definition and rules text for the "Master of Armour" Warlord Trait in the Warlord Traits section, specifically for use with the Armoured Spearhead Rite of War (applicable to Sicaran/Predator/Kratos).  
- Ensure the trait details the benefits given to these tanks when acting as the Warlord.   
- Cross-reference the trait in the Armoured Spearhead section so that its application is clear.  

**References and context**  
- Panoptica.txt (search for "Rite of War: Armoured Spearhead" and "Master of Armour").  
- Excerpt from Panoptica test1.txt, lines 923–940:
```
Rite of War: Armoured Spearhead (Page 102)
Balance Change
Replace the fourth Effects bullet point to read as follows:
"In a Detachment using this Rite of War, a single Legion Sicaran Squadron, Legion Predator Squadron, or Legion Kratos Squadron, composed of only a single model, must be selected as a HQ choice. This model gains the Master of the Legion and Legiones Astartes Command Tank Special Rules and must be selected as the army's Warlord and must use the Master of Armour Warlord Trait (as follows). In this case, the model must be selected as the Warlord despite having the Vehicle Unit Type, and regardless of any other rules that would require another model to be selected as the Warlord (note this means that some units and Characters cannot be selected as part of this army, such as Primarchs)."
```

**Impact**  
This is required for clarity and rules consistency for both building and playing armies using this Rite of War. Without it, Kratos (and others) using Armoured Spearhead have ambiguous Warlord abilities.
