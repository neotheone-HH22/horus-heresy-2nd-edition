Foreword
When the HH: Age of Darkness Discord Partner server’s moderation team first saw the
playtest rules for Warhammer: The Horus Heresy’s second edition, we were overjoyed that
our favourite tabletop game was getting the attention and love it deserved. We also were keenly
aware that with such an undertaking, there would be a few clarifications required for a handful
of rules, and some units would unfortunately be left on the cutting room floor. Some of these
units were fan-favourites, though - and so we decided it would be an undertaking we would
happily expend effort ourselves to remedy. Our first effort - which was internally known as
Operation: Retrograde during development - was the result.
This document is intended to stand alongside the great works of The Warhammer: Age of
Darkness team, providing additional units, options, and more for the Mechanicum and their
Divisio Tacticas - though you will still need a copy of the core Warhammer: Age of Darkness
Rulebook, as well as one of the various Liber or PDF faction books, as well as the PDFs
published via Warhammer Community, to make use of them.
We’re keenly aware that the rules in this book may, now or in the future, be rendered obsolete
by way of Games Workshop releasing official rules for any of the items contained within this
document. If that is the case, it should be assumed that those rules supersede these - unless you
agree otherwise with your opponent!
We hope you enjoy these rules, and Warhammer: The Horus Heresy!
The HH: Age of Darkness Discord Partner Server and Liber Panoptica Development Teams

Introduction
Within this document, you’ll find a mixture of new generic units and options available to all
Taghmata Omnissiah armies, as well as new Divisio Tactica-specific units and options. These
are all intended to represent concepts from the lore, resurrected units from previous editions
of the game, and more!
All of the units and options contained within this document were extensively play-tested by a
dedicated team of community writers from the Liber Panoptica Team. We recommend you use
the Liber Panoptica FAQ, Errata, and Balance Changes whilst using these units, for the best
game-play experience - and we all hope you enjoy using our supplements in your games of
The Horus Heresy!

the forces of the Taghmata

should their Archmagos fall,

behind…

Arkhan Land was a name well
known, if not well liked,
among almost all of the
Mechanicum. A peerless
techno-archaeologist, Land's
work on recovering
innumerable STC patterns
made him a practical celebrity
among the Legiones Astartes
and Martian Priesthood both
- but all who met him agreed
he was difficult to work with
or even like at the best of
times.
Land was accompanied at all
times by his simulacrum,
Sapien; a biologic-machine
hybrid which was markedly
not a servitor, in that it
retained far too much
intelligence and free will to be
considered one - though like
its master, it spent far too
much time annoying others,
and little else.
Unusually among the
Mechanicum, Land eschewed
outward augmentation,
preferring to keep his more
inward and understated. His
love of archaeotech, however,
was evident in his choice of
sidearm - a unique pistol that
all but disintegrated his foes
in barely a heartbeat.

MECHANICUM UNIT TYPES
This document includes a number of new unit Sub-types to represent an expanded and varied assembly of units from the Mechanicum at their
zenith. These Unit Types and Unit Sub-types are presented here for easy reference and operate under the rules established for Unit Types and Unit
Sub-types in the Horus Heresy: Age of Darkness Core Rulebook:
CRAWLER UNIT SUB-TYPE
Some of the more esoteric war-machines in the Mechancium’s arsenal went to war on great legs, and yet were not bipedal like the Dreadnoughts of the Legiones
Astartes and Imperial Army; these war machines were ponderous but indefatigable, and unceasing in their advance regardless of the terrain they trod.
The following rules apply to all models with the Crawler Sub-type:
• Any model with this Sub-type may fire Heavy and Ordnance weapons and counts as Stationary even if it moved in the preceding Movement phase.
• Any model with this Sub-type is never slowed by Difficult Terrain, and automatically passes Dangerous Terrain tests.
SERVITOR UNIT SUB-TYPE
Servitors represented the bulk of the Mechanicum’s labour force, but in times of war unique variants would be produced and mobilised. These warriors were
simple, without fear, but without self-determination too. In the face of the horrors of the Heresy, however, soldiers that obeyed without question were valued -
and even prized.
The following rules apply to all models with the Servitor Sub-type:
• Successful Wounds scored by attacks with the Poisoned or Fleshbane special rules must be re-rolled against models of the Servitor Sub-type.
• Any models with this Sub-type have the Fearless special rule.
• Any model with this Sub-type may fire all weapons they are equipped with in each Shooting Attack they make, including as part of a Reaction.
• Any model with this Sub-type may not use the Withdraw, Evade, or Hold the Line Reactions.
• Models with this Sub-type may fire Heavy weapons and count as Stationary even if they moved in the preceding Movement phase, and may
declare Charges as normal regardless of any Shooting Attacks made in the same turn.
SKITARII UNIT SUB-TYPE
The Skitarii were the standing forces of the Forge Worlds, and could be relied upon to protect and guard anything their masters deemed important. During the
Heresy, these forces were finally called to the front lines of battle - swiftly proving themselves capable in warfare.
The following rules apply to all models with the Skitarii Sub-type:
• Any infantry models with this Sub-type have the Feel No Pain (6+) Special Rule (unless they gain a better version of the Feel No Pain Special
Rule from any other source).
• Any Vehicle models with this Sub-type have the It Will Not Die (6+) Special Rule (unless they gain a better version of the It Will Not Die Special
Rule from any other source).
• Any models with this Sub-type may re-roll Feel No Pain Damage Mitigation rolls which are the result of attacks made by weapons with the
Poisoned (X) or Rad-Phage Special Rules.
• Any models with this Sub-type automatically have the Datasphere Uplink Special Rule.
• A Detachment may not include more models with the Skitarii Sub-Type than without unless the Compulsory HQ Choice in the same
Detachment has the Skitarii Unit Sub-type themselves.

Although a rare sight during the Great Crusade, as the years of the Heresy ground on it became increasingly common to see Skitarii leading the warhosts and

LEGIONES SKITARIIWARLORD TRAIT: UPLINK OVERRIDE
The War-Leader activates protocols usually reserved for commanding Magos, empowering his soldiers with loyalty and bravery – and the knowledge of

Special Rule (if they did not already have it) whilst your Warlord is alive.
In addition, an army whose Warlord has this Trait may make an additional Reaction during either one of the Opposing Player’s Movement or
Shooting phases (but not both), as long as the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.
